Reviews

Skipping straight to H3

Reviews from clients we have catered for over the last two seasons. We publish them as written, with the event type and month, and we do not edit them for length. If a review is critical we leave it up, because a page of unbroken praise tells a reader nothing they can use when they are choosing a caterer for something that matters to them. We ask every client the same three questions a fortnight after the event, once the relief has worn off and they can remember what actually went wrong. What did we get right, what would you change, and would you book us again. The answers go up here unedited. Several mention the same two things: that our staff cleared without being asked, and that the vegetarian counter was not an afterthought. A few mention queues at peak, which is fair, and is why we now split counters above four hundred guests. We do not solicit reviews with discounts, and we do not remove the unflattering ones, because a page of unbroken praise is worth nothing to somebody trying to decide whether to trust us with an event that matters. If you would like to speak to a past client directly rather than read a page of quotes, ask and we will put you in touch with two whose events resembled yours in size and format. We do that for most serious enquiries, and it tends to be more useful than anything we could write about ourselves. References are given with the client's permission, and we will not share a contact who has asked us not to. That policy costs us the occasional booking and we keep it anyway.
