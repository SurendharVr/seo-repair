What we do

Corporate catering built around the room it is served in. We plan the menu against the venue, the guest count and the clock, then cook and serve on site. Counters, plated service and buffets, staffed by our own team. Setup, service and clearance are ours, so the host stays with their guests. We have run events for ten people and for a thousand, in offices, convention halls and campus venues, and we bring the equipment with us every time. Most clients come back because the second event is easier than the first. Every event starts with a walkthrough of the room. We measure the service path, find the power, and decide where the counters go before we talk about food, because a menu that cannot be served hot at the far end of a hall is not a menu, it is a wish. From there we build the guest-count plan: how many chafers, how many staff, how long the queue runs at peak. We cook on site wherever the venue allows it, and we bring generators and water where it does not. Dietary requirements are collected in advance and cooked separately, not decanted from the main pans, and every tray is labelled. After service we clear, wash and remove everything we brought, and we leave the room as we found it. Clients get an itemised sheet afterwards showing what was consumed against what was ordered, which is how we get the next forecast closer.
