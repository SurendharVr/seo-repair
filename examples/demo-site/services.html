Service lines

Our service lines cover convention and delegate catering, executive dining, managed food-stall counters, corporate galas, high tea and daily cafeteria management. Each one has its own kit, its own staffing ratio and its own timing plan, because a delegate lunch for six hundred and a board lunch for twelve are not the same job wearing different clothes. We quote per head, itemised, with the staffing and equipment shown separately so nothing is buried. Convention and delegate catering runs on timing above all: six hundred people break for lunch at once and are back in session in forty minutes, so we stage counters to split the queue and pre-plate the slow items. Executive dining is the opposite problem, a small room where every plate is seen, so we send fewer staff but more senior ones. Managed food-stall counters run all day and need restocking rhythms rather than a single service peak. Corporate galas need a kitchen brief that survives a speech overrunning by twenty minutes. High tea and daily cafeteria management are volume work with tight unit economics, quoted monthly. Each line carries its own staffing ratio, its own equipment list and its own contingency, and we show all three on the quote so nothing is hidden inside a per-head number. Pricing is per head and itemised. Staffing, equipment hire and travel appear as their own lines rather than being folded into a single food cost, because a client comparing two quotes deserves to see where the difference actually sits. We hold prices for ninety days from the quote date, and we do not add surcharges after the fact for guest numbers inside the agreed tolerance band. Cancellations inside seventy-two hours are charged at cost of goods already committed, which we show you.
